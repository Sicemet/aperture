The Unmoved

the fire kneels · i am unmoved

The clutch from rage to authority. Fired at any rage-spike, waking or dreaming: see the glyph, run the descent line down, let the fire kneel into the vessel. Nothing is suppressed — the charge is relocated, head to heart, fury to weight. Every waking firing is a rep for the dream-side pivot.

Read top to bottom: crown of stilled flame, the charge drawn down, the vessel held inside the mountain, bench, ground, Night beneath. Five layers of seat under the flame.

The Reversal

i hold the look · the current feeds me

The trap’s jaws. Fired at a fear-spike in paralysis or dream: no fight to wake — surrender the body, hold the look, let the current line run inward. A glyph is pre-verbal; there is nothing to jam. It fires whole or not at all.

The current enters from the corner where they stand and terminates inside the aperture. The harvest, drawn in reverse.

The Mint

i stand at the mint · the fear strikes my coin

The economy working — same trap, same current, same authority, outcome changed: what returns is coin. Fired at any money-fear spike: the bill landing, the balance-dread, the chase-urge, the flinch when asked your price. That spike is the collector at the door. See the glyph, take the seat, let the blow fall on the coin. Nothing is suppressed — the fear is used, and the harder it hits, the harder the coin is struck.

Read top to bottom: the old claim rides in and is cut before the seat — the claim severed, never the current. The figure stands upstream where value is raw metal; the fear-current falls as the hammer; the coin takes the strike and bears the crown. Bench, ground, Night beneath. Full basis: Foundations, on the Learn tab.

The Remint

i hold the loop · my coin runs home

The money-side jaws, the Mint’s named reversal — the Reversal’s skeleton with the silver line bent into a circuit. Fired at the moment of outflow: the bill paid, the swipe, the transfer, watching the number drop. Hold the loop, release the coin on terms — deliberate, sealed, dread withheld. The flinch was the old program’s signature on the drain; the loop is its cancellation.

Read it beside its sibling: same spine, same eye, same stance — but the eye bears the seal now, and your own coin exits the aperture, orbits the crown, and comes home through the gate it left, to be struck again. The spending, drawn in reverse. Naming law: every reversal carries its opposite’s name — the Mint strikes, the Remint re-strikes what returns.

Nightly, at threshold — headphones on: trace each once with the eyes, once with a finger in the air. Reversal first (the jaws), Unmoved second (the hand), Mint third (the coin), Remint fourth (the loop). Thirty seconds each. Stop when they appear whole without effort. Log firings in the record.

Foundations

The building blocks. A Foundation is the basis of a thing — why the workings aimed at it work at all. Learned deep, the most complex task starts on solid ground. Numbered, kept, growing.

I — The Basis of Money

First face: money is a consensus working. A dollar is a promise wearing a number; it holds value because billions run the belief with total commitment, daily, without knowing they practice. The axiom proven at planetary scale: a belief that is run does work. And the word confesses the rest — currency, that which runs. Current. Money is the waking body of the court’s own current: value moving between vessels, feeding where terms allow, harvesting where terms are silent.

Second face: money is born as debt. Every unit is issued as someone’s IOU — loaned into existence, the number created when a debt is signed. The ledger is older than the coin: Babylon ran debt-tablets and bond-service centuries before anyone struck silver. And the interest is issued never — principal without its interest is a built-in shortage. Someone must always be hungry, by design. That is architecture, not your failing: harvester architecture at civilizational scale, a binding on future hours with no release clause you signed.

The fear is the collector. Scarcity-dread is the enforcement arm — the whip that keeps the current flowing toward the claim-holders. The flinch at the bill, the clench at the balance, the chase: that is the collector at the door, and the flinch is your signature on the invoice. Paid-out fear feeds the lack exactly as it fed the night visitors.

The cut: sever the claim, never the current. The flow was never the enemy; the direction and terms were. Cut the current and nothing moves — poverty with incense. The working severs the lien: the pre-attached claim that turns your value into someone else’s asset the moment it exists.

The upstream seat. Value exists before denomination — work, attention, word kept. Raw metal, yours at creation. The system reaches you only after your value is already coined as its IOU; the answer is positional: stand upstream, strike the value under your own seal — named, priced, owned — so it enters the current as your coin being spent, never their claim being serviced. When the collector comes to enforce the old arrangement, it walks into the snare and its own force strikes the next coin.

Paired workings: The Mint and The Remint, on the Court tab — the strike in, the loop out. Under any model — spirit, psyche, cashflow — the mechanism is the same: own what you make, name your price, stop paying fear into the lack, keep a channel open.

The Basics

Chaos magick, compressed to what you actually use. Read once, then practice; the app is the practice.

The one axiom

Belief is a tool, not a truth. You pick the model that produces results, run it with total commitment, and set it down when it stops paying. A belief that is run does work; a truth that is not run does nothing. Everything else here is engineering on top of that.

This means you never have to settle what is “really” happening. Psychological model, spirit model, energy model — run whichever gets results today. The ledger tab exists to track exactly that.

The method loop

INTENT → SIGIL → GNOSIS → FIRE → RELEASE → RECORD

State what you want. Compress it past the reach of the talking mind. Enter an altered state. Fire the compressed form through that state. Let go of it. Write down what happened. That loop, repeated, is the entire practice — everything else is variation.

Statement of intent

One sentence. Present tense, as if already true. Positive form only — name what IS, never what is not, because the deep mind drops negations. Be exact: a statement executes literally, like a contract. Vague intent produces vague results; unbounded intent binds more than you meant.

Weak: I want to stop being anxious. Strong: I AM CALM UNDER PRESSURE.

Sigil craft

The classic method (Austin Osman Spare): write the statement, strike out repeated letters — most also strike vowels — then fuse what remains into a single abstract glyph. Merge shared strokes, rotate letters, stack them, simplify. Finished when it no longer reads as writing and looks like it means something you cannot name.

Why it works, in any model you like: the statement passes to the deeper mind in a form the inner critic cannot argue with. The Craft tab does the striking for you and gives you the surface to draw on.

Gnosis

Intent alone does nothing; it is fired through an altered state. Two doors. Inhibitory: stillness — slow breathing, fixed gaze, the edge of sleep, sensory quiet. Excitatory: peak — exhaustion, dance, intense emotion caught at its crest. Use whichever door opens easiest for you, and note that the edge of sleep is one of the most reliable gates there is.

The breath box on the Craft tab is the humble, always-available inhibitory route. It is enough.

Charging & firing

Enter gnosis with the glyph at hand. At the deepest point, look at it once — total attention, no words — then stop. That look is the firing. For a result-in-the-world sigil, the classic next move is to forget it: destroy the paper, bury the file, move your attention decisively elsewhere so the deep mind runs it undisturbed.

The exception: a sigil you must fire in the moment — a calm-trigger, a courage mark — is overlearned instead: traced daily until it draws itself, so it comes when called with no paper needed.

Banishing & closing

Every working opens a charged space and must close it. Simplest banishing: laughter — a genuine laugh at the whole affair collapses the charge cleanly. Or a fixed phrase and gesture: palms together, it is done, the space is closed. Same words every time, so the cue itself gains weight.

Closing is not superstition; it is hygiene. It stops the working from leaking into the rest of your day, and it stops lust-for-result — the anxious checking that strangles outcomes.

Servitors, in brief

A servitor is a bounded thoughtform with a job: named, given a duty, fed attention on a schedule, and dismissed or retired when done. Build one only after the sigil loop is comfortable. Three clauses every servitor needs: a duty (one job, stated exactly), a feed (what it lawfully receives, and when), and a release (the condition that ends it). An instrument with no release clause binds its maker.

Paradigms & the ledger

A paradigm is a whole belief-frame worn on purpose — run fully while in, set down cleanly when out. The skill is the clutch, not the frame. The Ledger tab treats each belief as an experiment: state it, run it, log results, then adopt, keep testing, or retire it. Nothing is held sacred; everything is held accountable.

The record

This practice is empirical or it is nothing. Every working gets a log entry: date, what you did, what state you reached, what happened after. Memory flatters and distorts; a written record is the only honest instrument you have. One success is an anecdote. A log is a practice.
